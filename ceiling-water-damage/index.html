Ceiling Water Damage Repair Help in Redding

When a brown circle finally appears above you, the leak has been at work for a while. Long before you see discoloration, the insulation is wet, moisture has followed the framing and a puddle sits on the hidden side of the panel. The leak may be a bathroom on the floor above, a pipe in the ceiling, an air conditioner drain line or the roof. We trace it to the source before anything gets patched.

Painting over the stain does not fix it. Moisture left in the cavity brings the discoloration back and lets mold get going on the paper side in 24 to 48 hours. Our crew removes no more ceiling than necessary, takes out soaked insulation, dries the framing and the drywall that stays, and finishes with a patch, texture and paint that match. If the ceiling has started to droop, treat it differently. Stay out from under it and call.

What It Looks Like

  • Thin cracking in the ceiling finish centered on a wet looking area
  • One room that smells damp even though nothing looks wet
  • Joint tape coming loose, with screws and panel edges telegraphing through
  • Water dripping from a light fixture, vent or smoke detector
  • Bits of textured finish falling to the floor below a damp patch
  • Patches of spotty discoloration that look like mold on the ceiling
The Clock

What Happens if You Wait

The first few hours

Water Spreads Above

Water runs along joists and soaks into insulation. Because the panel soaks from its top side, nothing may look wrong from underneath.

24 to 48 hours

Marks Start Showing

A stain forms and paint starts to blister. Mold can begin on the drywall paper and the wet insulation presses down on the panel.

Days 3 through 7

Sagging Sets In

The panel turns soft and starts slipping off its fasteners. Between the joists the surface dips, joints split apart and a musty smell arrives below.

After a week

Sections May Fall

Waterlogged sections can fall, bringing wet insulation with them. Mold is established in the cavity and the repair now covers a much larger area.

Behind the Surface

Risks That Are Easy to Miss

Mold Above You

Even when the ceiling feels dry to the touch, the batts and the unseen face of the drywall remain damp, giving mold a hidden place to spread.

Sudden Collapse

Once saturated, drywall weighs much more and no longer holds its screws well. An entire piece can fall from a bulging spot onto anything below.

Older Ceiling Materials

Popcorn texture and plaster from past decades may have asbestos in them. If your home has one, it should be tested before anyone scrapes, cuts or tears out a damaged area.

Water Travels Sideways

The wet spot is often not below the leak, since water rides pipes, ducts and joists before it falls. We check beyond the visible stain so soaked sections down the line are not overlooked.

What We Do

What Happens After You Call

1

Trace the Leak

The first job is locating the starting point, which involves meter readings, a thermal camera and a look at whatever is over the ceiling. Until the water stops coming, fixing the ceiling would not last.

2

Secure the Room

Power to wet fixtures is shut off and furniture below is covered or moved. Where water is trapped overhead, it gets drained under control into containers.

3

Remove and Dry Out

Wet insulation is removed along with any drywall that cannot stay. The joists and nearby ceiling are dried with air movers plus a dehumidifier, and antimicrobial goes on wherever the water was dirty.

4

Repair and Finish

After the meter shows normal levels, new insulation and drywall go up, joints are taped and finished, the texture is matched and a stain blocking primer goes on ahead of paint.
