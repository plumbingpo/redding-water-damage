Preventing Sewage Backups in Redding, CA

Home / Blog / Preventing Sewage Backups in Redding, CA

A sewage backup is one of the most unpleasant emergencies a homeowner can face. Raw sewage entering your living spaces creates immediate health hazards, causes extensive property damage, and requires professional cleanup that can cost thousands of dollars. Learn the reasons these backups happen, act on a few preventive measures, and you cut the odds considerably. Redding Restoration wrote this guide to walk Redding, CA owners through the usual causes and some practical ways to keep a property protected.

What Causes a Sewer to Back Up

Sewage backups occur when wastewater cannot flow from your property to the municipal sewer system or septic tank as intended. Most cases trace back to the following: sewer pipes that have aged or collapsed, roots from trees intruding into the pipe, grease, hair, and debris clogging the line, heavy rainfall overloading the municipal sewers, a combined sewer overflow event, or improper items going down the toilet, including excessive paper, wipes, and feminine products.

Tree Roots in the Sewer Line

Tree roots are one of the most common causes of sewer line blockages in Redding. A sewer pipe is a steady supply of moisture, which is exactly what roots go looking for. Entry happens at connection points, cracks, and joints, and over time the growth packs the pipe, creating the blockages behind many backups. The risk is highest with aging clay tile pipe, which roots penetrate readily. Schedule a sewer camera inspection every 2 to 3 years so root trouble is found ahead of any backup.

Prevention Strategies

Protecting your Redding home from sewage backups involves several preventive measures: install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent municipal system backflows. Grease and cooking oil should never go down a drain. Only flush toilet paper, never wipes, feminine products, or other items. Routine inspection and cleaning of your sewer line should be on the calendar. A drain that is running slow needs prompt attention, or it may block completely. Find out where your sewer cleanout is so it can be reached in an emergency. Think about adding a sewage backup endorsement to your homeowner's policy, or buying sewer line insurance.

What to Do If Sewage Backs Up

If you experience a sewage backup, do not attempt cleanup yourself. Serious illness can come from the dangerous pathogens in sewage, among them parasites, bacteria, and viruses.
